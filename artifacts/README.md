# Neural Vocoder Experimental Studies

This collection reports the controlled evaluation of efficient neural vocoders on LJSpeech 1.1.

| Study | Scientific focus | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `study_1/` | Budget-controlled training reproduction | Twelve project-trained baselines, seed-specific evaluations, training histories, and baseline comparisons |
| `study_2/` | Deployment optimization | Same-lane compilation, precision, quantization, pruning, exported-runtime, and ODE-step interventions |
| `study_3/` | Cross-model synthesis | Fidelity–efficiency frontiers, hardware deployment measurements, and intervention-level conclusions |

Each study states its dataset partition, measurement protocol, result boundaries, and limitations alongside the corresponding tables, logs, configurations, and figures.
