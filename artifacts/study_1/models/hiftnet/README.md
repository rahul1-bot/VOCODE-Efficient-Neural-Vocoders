# HiFTNet

HiFTNet is a documented Study 1 exclusion. Its F0-dependent conditioning and training path were not completed under the locked common harness, so no admissible project-trained checkpoint or three-seed evaluation exists.

`experiments.csv` is intentionally empty. `train/` and `test/` record the absence of admitted executions, while `figures/` visualizes the resulting eligibility status. HiFTNet is not counted among the twelve measured Study 1 models.
